Conclusion

The bot found the setup-then-strike pattern a human might take hours to discover

RL surpasses human baseline (35% vs ~20%) on ante 1 — without any explicit strategy encoding
Winning games follow a pattern: bank chips with two_pair, then burn discards to land a flush or straight
Hunting is worth it — aggressive games clear with 25% more headroom
Next: add jokers and the shop layer — where Balatro's real complexity begins
